    🎛 Mixing Mode & Weight
    Overlap Mode: Both files are multiplied by their weights and summed sample-by-sample. Result length = longest audio. The shorter file is zero-padded to match. At 50% the classifier should blend toward both contributing dialects proportionally.
    Sequential Mode: No overlap — the slider controls how much of Audio A (from its start) is followed by how much of Audio B (from its end). Result length = longest audio. Remaining time is padded with silence.
